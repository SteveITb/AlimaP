About

Driving Kenya's Water Future

Engineer Samuel Alima serves as the Water Secretary for the State Department of Water and Sanitation under the Ministry of Water, Sanitation and Irrigation of the Republic of Kenya. He brings over two decades of technical expertise and public service leadership to the role.

His mandate encompasses policy formulation, oversight of major infrastructure projects, coordination with county governments, and implementation of Kenya's Water Security agenda in alignment with Vision 2030 and the Big Four Agenda.

A registered engineer with a distinguished academic background, Eng. Alima has led transformative interventions in water supply, irrigation, and sanitation across Kenya — from arid and semi-arid regions to rapidly urbanising counties.
